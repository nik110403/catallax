# Catallax — PRODUCT.md

**What it is:** a self-hosted financial terminal for one person's money and the markets
around it — net worth, holdings, ledger, macro, cross-asset research, equity screening, a
quant lab, and a paper-trading desk, running in Docker on the owner's own machine.

**Audience:** one person (and optionally friends they explicitly share with via a
passphrase gate) — not a multi-tenant product, no signup flow, no public audience.

**Register: operate** (terminal/dashboard), not brand/marketing. Five permanent sections
(Home, Portfolio, Markets, Quant, Live), keyboard-driven (⌘K), dense-by-design: "numbers
per square inch, not whitespace" (README). Dark theme only for beta.

**Tone:** Bloomberg-shaped, information-dense, no onboarding funnel, no persuasive copy.
